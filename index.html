I am currently a Post-Doc at the CRC "Negation in Language and Beyond" (NegLaB) at Goethe University Frankfurt. I am a theoretical linguist and am passionate about the most abstract and profound aspects of our linguistic knowledge, those that account for the universal properties of human languages, and for the way in which they can differ from each other. I specialize in the study of morphosyntactic variation via a combination of traditional fieldwork, experimental methods, and computational modeling of data coming from corpora and typological databases. I am particularly interested in exploring computational methods as a way to probe formal theories of syntax and syntactic variation.
