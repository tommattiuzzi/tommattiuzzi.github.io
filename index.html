I am a Post-Doc at the CRC "Negation in Language and Beyond" (NegLaB) at Goethe University Frankfurt.

I am a theoretical linguist and am drawn to the question of what underlies the universal properties of human languages and what drives their variation. My work focuses on morphosyntactic variation, combining traditional fieldwork, experimental methods, and computational modeling of corpus and typological data to probe formal theories of syntax.

My research interests

(Morpho)syntactic theory, Syntactic micro- and macro-variation, Computational modeling of linguistic competence and variation
